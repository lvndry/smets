# Name ideas

None of these has been checked against GitHub, crates.io, PyPI, Hugging Face or trademarks. Do that before choosing.

Wanted: short, easy to type as a crate and CLI name, says "pulling a mix apart", not tied to Euphoria or to DJs (useful to anyone).

## Pulling apart
- **tine**: a prong of a fork, one strand out of a handle. Short, crate-friendly.
- **strand**: one thread out of a rope. Reads well in "strand separates a track into strands".
- **unmix**: plain and obvious, but likely taken (Open-Unmix).
- **unbake**: you cannot unbake a cake, which is the joke. Memorable, slightly cheeky.
- **untwist**, **untangle**: friendly verbs.
- **prise**: to pry apart.
- **sift**: separating fine from coarse.
- **riffle**: shuffling cards, also a music pun.

## Stems and parts
- **stemwise**, **stemwell**, **stemline**: stem-based, descriptive.
- **openstems**: says exactly what it is, hard to own as a brand.
- **leaf**, **sprig**: stem and plant words.
- **splitwood**, **cleave**: stronger verbs.

## Sound and openness
- **clearcut**: clean separation.
- **daylight**: letting light between the parts.
- **fathom**, **tarn**, **ledger**: standalone words, no meaning attached.
- **sunder**: archaic "split apart", short and distinctive.

## Shortlist (my picks)
1. **tine**: shortest, good CLI, not a music brand.
2. **strand**: clear image, easy to explain, probably crowded as a word.
3. **sunder**: distinctive and short, a little harsh.
4. **stemwell**: says stems, sounds like a product.
